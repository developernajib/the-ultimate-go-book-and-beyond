## 88.10 Core Concepts Recap

### Key Takeaways

The following points capture the most important lessons from this chapter. If you remember nothing else, remember that writing is a multiplier for your technical skills - it turns individual expertise into organizational knowledge that persists long after you move on to other projects.

1. **Writing scales your impact** - Documents reach everyone, code reaches one team
2. **Start with the conclusion** - Pyramid structure, front-load important info
3. **Know your audience** - Adjust detail level and terminology
4. **RFCs build consensus** - The process matters as much as the document
5. **Document decisions** - ADRs create institutional memory
6. **Keep it maintainable** - Outdated docs are worse than no docs

### Writing Checklist

Use this checklist as a final review step before sharing any technical document. Going through each item takes only a few minutes but catches the most common problems that cause documents to stall in review or fail to communicate their intent clearly.

- [ ] Clear problem statement
- [ ] Conclusion up front
- [ ] Audience-appropriate detail
- [ ] Alternatives considered
- [ ] Trade-offs acknowledged
- [ ] Actionable next steps
- [ ] Easy to skim (headings, lists)
- [ ] Proofread for clarity
