## Learning Objectives

After completing this chapter, you will be able to:

1. **Understand chaos engineering principles** and the scientific method behind controlled failure injection
2. **Implement fault injection** at every system layer (HTTP, gRPC, database, network)
3. **Build chaos experiment frameworks** with proper safety controls and rollback mechanisms
4. **Create chaos monkey patterns** for continuous resilience testing in production
5. **Design steady-state verification** using metrics to validate system health
6. **Integrate chaos testing** into CI/CD pipelines with automated experiments
7. **Establish post-mortem culture** to learn from failures and improve systems

### Detailed Outcomes

**Senior engineer**

- Design and run chaos experiments with hypotheses and success criteria.
- Lead GameDay exercises.
- Translate chaos findings into resilience improvements.

**Staff or Principal**

- Establish org-wide chaos engineering program.
- Drive cultural adoption.
- Make chaos testing part of production readiness.
