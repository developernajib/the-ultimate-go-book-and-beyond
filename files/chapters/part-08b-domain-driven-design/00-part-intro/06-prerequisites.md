## Prerequisites and Target Audience

### Technical Prerequisites

Before reading this part, you should be comfortable with:

- Intermediate to advanced Go (interfaces, goroutines, error handling, testing)
- Basic database concepts (PostgreSQL)
- REST and gRPC API patterns (covered in Parts VI and VII)
- Microservices fundamentals (covered in Part VIII)

---


### Who This Part Is For

This part is primarily aimed at mid-level and senior Go engineers who are building or maintaining complex, business-critical systems. If you are working on a side project or a simple CRUD API, DDD may be more overhead than you need - and we will say so explicitly in the chapters. But if you are working on a system with:

- Complex business rules that keep changing
- Multiple teams working on the same codebase
- A domain that experts care deeply about and describe in rich, specific language
- Requirements for auditability, event history, or temporal queries
- Plans to scale to the complexity of a large product

...then DDD is exactly the tool you need, and this part will give you the complete implementation knowledge to apply it in Go.
