## Problem Statement

In this problem, you are asked to design a music streaming platform similar to Spotify. The challenge is not just playing audio files. It involves handling massive scale, personalized recommendations, real-time playback across devices, and complex royalty calculations. Understanding the breadth of features helps you scope the design and prioritize the most critical components during an interview.

Design Spotify supporting:
- 600M users, 80M songs, 5B playlists
- Streaming audio at multiple bitrates (96, 160, 320 kbps) plus lossless FLAC for Premium
- Search by song, artist, album, podcast
- Personalized recommendations (Discover Weekly)
- Offline downloads (premium users)
- Social features: collaborative playlists, listening activity
- Podcast support with chapter markers
- 99.99% streaming availability
