## Interview Scoring Rubric

Interviewers typically evaluate system design answers against a leveling rubric. Understanding what is expected at each level helps you calibrate the depth of your answer. An L4 (mid-level) candidate should cover the basics, while an L6 (senior/staff) candidate is expected to discuss nuanced trade-offs like trending algorithms and fan-out economics.

**L4:** Tweet + timeline + DB. Fan-out concept. Read-heavy understanding.

**L5:** Hybrid fan-out. Snowflake IDs. Redis sorted set. Celebrity thundering herd. Search pipeline.

**L6:** Trending with z-score velocity. Fan-out economics. Rate limiting. Algorithmic timeline. Kafka partitioning.
