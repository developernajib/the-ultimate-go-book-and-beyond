## Interview Scoring Rubric

This rubric shows what interviewers expect at different seniority levels. An L4 (mid-level) candidate should cover the basics, while an L5 (senior) candidate needs to discuss scaling and data partitioning in depth. An L6 (staff) candidate is expected to demonstrate deep knowledge of cryptographic protocols, compliance considerations, and the operational reality of running such a system at scale.

**L4:** WebSocket architecture. Offline storage. Basic delivery.

**L5:** Redis presence. Offline queue flush. Group fan-out. Cassandra partitions. Message ordering. E2E mentioned.

**L6:** Signal Protocol (X3DH + Double Ratchet). Multi-device E2E. Delivery receipts. 100B msg/day with 50 engineers. Regulatory compliance.
