## Problem Statement

WhatsApp handles 100 billion messages per day with roughly 50 engineers, a testament to strong infrastructure design. In a system design interview, the challenge lies in supporting billions of users with sub-second message delivery, end-to-end encryption, and offline message queuing.

Design WhatsApp supporting:
- 2 billion users, 100 billion messages/day
- Real-time delivery: sub-second end to end, < 100ms of server-side processing
- End-to-end encryption
- Group chats (up to 1024 members)
- Media sharing (photos, videos, documents)
- Message status: sent → delivered → read
- Offline message queuing
- Voice and video calls
