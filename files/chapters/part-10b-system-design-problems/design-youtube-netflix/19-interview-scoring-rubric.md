## Interview Scoring Rubric

This rubric reflects how FAANG-level interviewers typically evaluate video streaming system design answers. Each level builds on the previous one, so an L5 answer must include everything from L4 plus additional depth. Use this to gauge your own preparation and identify which areas need more study.

**L4:** Correct high-level architecture. Mentions HLS, S3, Redis. Covers happy path.

**L5:** Transcoding pipeline deep dive. Presigned URLs. CDN cache hit rates. Two-stage recommendations. Failure handling. Capacity-driven decisions.

**L6:** Netflix vs YouTube comparison. Per-title encoding. CDN economics. View count pipeline. Content moderation. DRM architecture. Live streaming extension.
