## 96C.1 Pre-Resignation Preparation

### The Pre-Resignation Checklist


Resigning without preparation is one of the most expensive mistakes engineers make. Missed vesting dates, forfeited bonuses, and lost documentation can cost tens of thousands of dollars. Complete every item on this checklist before you schedule the resignation conversation.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                BEFORE YOU RESIGN: CRITICAL CHECKLIST             │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                  │
│  FINANCIAL ITEMS                                                 │
│  □ Check equity vesting schedule                                 │
│  □ Review bonus timing and eligibility                           │
│  □ Understand benefits expiration dates                          │
│  □ Calculate COBRA costs if needed                               │
│  □ Plan 401k rollover                                            │
│  □ Check stock option exercise deadlines                         │
│                                                                  │
│  DOCUMENTATION                                                   │
│  □ Save performance reviews (you own these)                      │
│  □ Document your achievements                                    │
│  □ Get copies of awards/recognition                              │
│  □ Export LinkedIn recommendations                               │
│  □ Save contact information for colleagues                       │
│                                                                  │
│  NEW JOB CONFIRMED                                               │
│  □ Offer letter signed                                           │
│  □ Background check cleared                                      │
│  □ Start date confirmed in writing                               │
│                                                                  │
│  PERSONAL ITEMS                                                  │
│  □ Remove personal files from work computer                      │
│  □ Forward important personal emails                             │
│  □ Update emergency contacts                                     │
│                                                                  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

CRITICAL: Do NOT resign until your new job background check clears.
Offers can be rescinded. Don't burn bridges prematurely.
```

### Understanding Your Vesting Schedule

Equity vesting schedules create natural departure windows. Leaving even a few weeks before a vesting date can mean forfeiting significant money. Most new employers will accommodate a delayed start if you explain the financial reasoning.

```
EQUITY VESTING CONSIDERATIONS:

Scenario: You have RSUs vesting quarterly

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  TODAY: October 15                                               │
│  NEXT VEST: November 15 ($25,000)                                │
│  NEW JOB START: November 1                                       │
│                                                                  │
│  OPTION A: Start November 1                                      │
│  Result: Forfeit $25,000 in RSUs                                 │
│                                                                  │
│  OPTION B: Negotiate November 20 start date                      │
│  Result: Capture $25,000 vest, start 3 weeks later               │
│                                                                  │
│  MATH: 3 weeks delay = $25,000 value                             │
│  That's over $433,000 annualized value for waiting               │
│                                                                  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

KEY DATES TO CHECK:
- Next RSU/option vest date
- Annual bonus payment date
- Equity cliff date (if applicable)
- Benefits renewal date

NEGOTIATION: New employers often accommodate delayed starts
for significant vesting events. Just ask.
```

### Bonus Timing Awareness

Annual bonuses at most technology companies are paid in Q1 for the previous year's performance. Resigning before the payment date typically means forfeiting the entire bonus, even if you worked the full performance year. Timing your departure around this date can be worth tens of thousands of dollars.

```
BONUS TIMING STRATEGY:

Most companies pay annual bonuses in Q1 (Jan-March)
for the previous year's performance.

TYPICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Must be employed on payment date
- Or must be employed on Dec 31
- Check your specific policy in offer letter/handbook

EXAMPLE SCENARIOS:

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  SCENARIO 1: Resign in November                                  │
│  Status: Worked 11 months of performance year                    │
│  Bonus: $0 (not employed on payment date)                        │
│  LOSS: Full bonus ($30,000+)                                     │
│                                                                  │
│  SCENARIO 2: Resign in March (after bonus payment)               │
│  Status: Received full bonus for previous year                   │
│  Bonus: $30,000 received                                         │
│  GAIN: Full bonus captured                                       │
│                                                                  │
│  BETTER STRATEGY:                                                │
│  - Start job search in Q4                                        │
│  - Interview in Jan/Feb                                          │
│  - Accept offer with March/April start                           │
│  - Receive bonus, then resign                                    │
│                                                                  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

CAUTION: Some companies have clawback clauses if you leave
within X days of bonus payment. Read the fine print.
```

### Stock Option Exercise Planning

If you hold stock options (ISOs or NSOs), your exercise window typically begins at termination and closes 90 days later. Missing this deadline means your vested options expire worthless. The financial and tax implications of exercising can be substantial, so plan this well before your resignation date.

```
STOCK OPTIONS: CRITICAL TIMELINE

If you have ISOs (Incentive Stock Options):
- You typically have 90 days after termination to exercise
- After 90 days: Options expire worthless
- Exercise requires cash: (shares × strike price)

EXERCISE DECISION FRAMEWORK:

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  YOUR SITUATION:                                                 │
│  Vested options: 10,000 shares                                   │
│  Strike price: $5                                                │
│  Current 409A value: $25                                         │
│  Exercise cost: $50,000                                          │
│  Paper value: $250,000                                           │
│  Paper gain: $200,000                                            │
│                                                                  │
│  QUESTIONS TO ASK:                                               │
│                                                                  │
│  1. Is the company likely to have a liquidity event?             │
│     (IPO, acquisition within 2-3 years)                          │
│                                                                  │
│  2. Can you afford to lose the exercise cost?                    │
│     ($50,000 is gone if company fails)                           │
│                                                                  │
│  3. What are the tax implications?                               │
│     - ISO: AMT may apply on $200k spread                         │
│     - NSO: $200k taxed as ordinary income                        │
│                                                                  │
│  4. Did you file 83(b) election? (affects tax treatment)         │
│                                                                  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

CONSULT A TAX ADVISOR before making large exercise decisions.
The tax implications can be substantial.
```

### Document Collection

Once you resign, your access to company systems will eventually be revoked. Collect all documents that are legally yours - performance reviews, self-assessments, recognition emails, and colleague contact information - before you give notice. Never take proprietary code or confidential data.

```

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  PERFORMANCE DOCUMENTATION                                       │

│  ├── Promotion documents                                         │
│  ├── Award/recognition emails                                    │
│  └── Positive feedback emails (from managers, peers)             │
│                                                                  │
│  ACHIEVEMENT EVIDENCE                                            │
```

### Junior → FAANG: Pre-Resignation Preparation as Risk Management

The pre-resignation checklist is primarily a risk management exercise. Each item removes a specific category of risk. Reviewing your employment agreement removes the risk of accidentally violating legal terms. Confirming your start date in writing removes the risk of resigning before having a confirmed offer. Organizing personal files removes the risk of losing access to personal documents when your accounts are deactivated.

The most commonly skipped preparation item is backing up personal contacts from your work email and messaging systems before giving notice. Once you resign, your access to these systems can be revoked within hours at some companies, particularly in the engineering and technology sector. A list of personal contact information for colleagues whose relationship you want to maintain is worth 30 minutes of preparation effort before the resignation conversation.

For Go engineers specifically: review any code you have written that you consider part of your personal portfolio. Your employment agreement almost certainly assigns IP in work-related code to the employer, but understanding what is clearly employer IP versus what might be considered pre-existing personal knowledge is worth a brief review before you start work that might look similar at your new employer.

### Senior @ FAANG: Senior Pre-Resignation Governance and the Transition Document

At senior level, the pre-resignation preparation extends to creating a formal transition document. This is a structured record of: every system you own or co-own (with architectural context), every external relationship you maintain on behalf of the company (vendor contacts, partner engineers, open-source community relationships), every ongoing initiative in flight and its current status, and every critical decision that will need to be made in the next 60 days and who should make it.

This document is a professional investment in the organization you are leaving and a personal investment in your reputation as someone who leaves organizations stronger than they found them. Senior engineers known for producing thorough transition documents receive strong references and are sometimes hired back at higher levels. The time investment is four to eight hours over your last week and the reputation return is substantial.

The governance check before resignation: confirm that you are not violating any pending equity cliff or vesting milestone in a way that is financially significant. Know your exact unvested equity schedule, the cliff date for any refresh grants, and the treatment of unvested equity under your company's policies in the event of voluntary termination.
