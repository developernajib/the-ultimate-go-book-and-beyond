## 96C.7 Last Day Logistics

### Equipment and Access

Your last day involves returning company property, revoking system access, and tying up administrative loose ends. Most companies have a formal offboarding process, but it often misses things. Use this checklist to make sure nothing is forgotten.

```
LAST DAY CHECKLIST:

EQUIPMENT TO RETURN:
□ Laptop and charger
□ External monitors
□ Keyboard/mouse
□ Badge/access cards
□ Parking pass
□ Company credit card
□ Any other company property

ACCESS THAT WILL BE REVOKED:
□ Email (usually immediate)
□ Slack/Teams
□ Code repositories
□ Internal systems
□ VPN
□ Building access

BEFORE ACCESS IS CUT:
□ Export important contacts to personal email
□ Download personal files (photos, non-work docs)
□ Note down any personal info stored in work accounts
□ Screenshot any important achievement records
□ Get copies of your work you can legally keep

IMPORTANT: Don't copy code, proprietary info, or customer data.
This violates policy and potentially law.
```

### Farewell Communication

A good farewell email leaves a positive final impression and provides your personal contact information to colleagues you want to stay in touch with. Keep it short, warm, and professional. This email will be the last thing many colleagues read from you.

```
FAREWELL EMAIL TEMPLATE:

Subject: Farewell and Thank You

Hi everyone,

Today is my last day at [Company], and I wanted to take
a moment to say thank you.

The past [X years] have been an incredible journey. I've
had the privilege of working with amazing people, tackling
interesting problems, and growing both professionally and
personally.

[Optional: 1-2 sentences about specific highlight]

I'm excited about my next chapter, but I'll miss this team.

Let's stay in touch:
- Personal email: [email]
- LinkedIn: [link]

Thank you for everything. I wish you all continued success.

Best,
[Your Name]

---

NOTES:
- Keep it brief and positive
- Don't mention where you're going (optional)
- Send to relevant distribution list (not all-company)
- CC your personal email to have a copy
```

### Maintaining Connections

Your professional network is one of the most valuable assets you build during any job. Former colleagues become future referrers, co-founders, and clients. Invest a few minutes on your last day to connect on LinkedIn and exchange personal contact information with the people who matter to you.

```
STAYING CONNECTED:

BEFORE YOU LEAVE:
□ Connect with colleagues on LinkedIn
□ Exchange personal email addresses with close colleagues
□ Save manager's personal contact for references
□ Note down people you want to stay in touch with

AFTER YOU LEAVE:
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  FIRST MONTH:                                                    │
│  ├── Settle into new role                                        │
│  ├── Send "landed safely" update to close colleagues             │
│  └── Accept LinkedIn requests                                    │
│                                                                  │
│  FIRST QUARTER:                                                  │
│  ├── Coffee/lunch with former close colleagues                   │
│  ├── Respond to any transition questions helpfully               │
│  └── Engage with their posts on LinkedIn                         │
│                                                                  │
│  ONGOING:                                                        │
│  ├── Annual catch-up with key contacts                           │
│  ├── Congratulate on their achievements                          │
│  ├── Attend alumni events (if company has them)                  │
│  └── Help with referrals when appropriate                        │

- Former colleagues = future references
- Industry connections lead to opportunities
- Good people end up at good companies

```

### Junior → FAANG: Last Day as the Final Chapter of Your Professional Story at This Company

Your last day is the most visible day of your notice period and the one people remember most. It is worth investing effort to make it a genuinely positive experience rather than a relief or an awkward conclusion.

Send individual messages to five to ten colleagues whose relationship matters to you. Not a mass email: individual, personal notes that reference something specific you appreciated or learned from each person. This takes 30 minutes and produces lasting goodwill.

Confirm with IT and HR that you understand what happens to your access, your email, and your pending pay. At some FAANG companies, email access terminates immediately on the last day. Know what to expect so you can share your personal contact information proactively before that happens.

### Senior @ FAANG: Last Day Governance and the Alumni Network Activation

At senior level, the last day includes an explicit alumni network activation step. The engineering community at FAANG companies maintains informal alumni networks that are valuable career resources. Making your departure known to the right people, in a positive framing, activates these networks in a way that benefits your future career.

The LinkedIn connection and announcement practice: update your LinkedIn profile on your last day (or shortly after) with a warm, forward-looking post that thanks the organization and your colleagues. This normalizes the transition, activates your network's awareness that you are open to conversations, and positions you professionally for the next chapter. It takes 15 minutes and is visible to every person in your professional network.

The governance checklist for last day: return all equipment, confirm your final paycheck date and equity settlement timeline, download any personal documents you need before access is revoked, and confirm your departure date with payroll. Keep your resignation letter and any written agreements from your notice period in your personal records.
