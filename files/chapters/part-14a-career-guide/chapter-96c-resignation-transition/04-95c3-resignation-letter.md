## 96C.3 Resignation Letter

### The Format

A resignation letter is a formal record, not a personal essay. Keep it brief, professional, and free of anything you would not want read aloud in a future reference check. The essentials are a clear statement that you are resigning, your last working day, a note of gratitude, and an offer to help with the transition.

```
RESIGNATION LETTER ESSENTIALS:

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                  │
│  LENGTH: 3-5 paragraphs, one page maximum                        │
│                                                                  │
│  FORMAT: Formal business letter                                  │
│                                                                  │
│  DELIVERY: Email is standard (physical letter outdated)          │
│                                                                  │
│  TONE: Professional, positive, gracious                          │
│                                                                  │
│  MUST INCLUDE:                                                   │
│  ├── Clear statement of resignation                              │
│  ├── Last working day                                            │
│  ├── Gratitude                                                   │
│  └── Offer to help with transition                               │
│                                                                  │
│  DO NOT INCLUDE:                                                 │
│  ├── Reasons for leaving                                         │
│  ├── Criticism of company/people                                 │
│  ├── Details of new job                                          │
│  ├── Salary information                                          │
│  └── Emotional content                                           │
│                                                                  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Resignation Letter Template

The resignation process must balance personal professionalism with legal obligations, ensuring a clean departure that neither burns bridges nor exposes the candidate to avoidable risk. The following preparation checklist and conversation templates cover the essential steps from securing the new offer in writing through the last day.

```
STANDARD RESIGNATION LETTER:

Subject: Resignation - [Your Name]

Dear [Manager's Name],

Please accept this letter as formal notification of my
resignation from my position as [Your Title] at [Company Name].
My last day of work will be [Date - typically 2 weeks out].

I want to express my sincere gratitude for the opportunities
I've had during my time here. I've grown significantly as
an engineer and have valued working with such a talented team.

During my remaining time, I'm committed to ensuring a smooth
transition. I'm happy to help document my projects, train
colleagues, and complete any urgent work.

Thank you again for everything. I wish you and the team
continued success.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[Date]

---

Note: Send this email AFTER your verbal conversation,
not before. The letter is a formality that follows the
in-person discussion.
```

### Senior/Leadership Resignation Template

Senior engineers and tech leads carry more institutional knowledge and usually have broader impact on in-flight projects. A longer, more detailed letter that acknowledges this responsibility and proposes a concrete transition plan shows maturity and helps maintain the relationship.

```
FOR SENIOR ROLES OR LONGER TENURE:

Subject: Resignation - [Your Name], Effective [Date]

Dear [Manager's Name],

After [X years] at [Company], I have made the difficult
decision to resign from my position as [Title]. My last
working day will be [Date].

This was not an easy decision. My time here has been
tremendously rewarding - from [specific project/achievement]
to [another highlight]. I'm proud of what we've built together.

I understand this transition comes at a busy time, and I'm
committed to making it as smooth as possible. I propose:

- Documenting all current projects and their status
- Creating runbooks for systems I own
- Training [colleague name] on [specific area]
- Being available for questions after departure if needed

I'll always be grateful for the mentorship, opportunities,
and friendships I've found here. I look forward to staying
in touch with many of you.

Thank you for everything.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

CC: [HR Representative - optional]
```

### What to CC and When

Who you include on your resignation email matters. CC the wrong people and you create awkward situations. Leave out the right people and HR may not process your departure on time. Follow the distribution rules below to keep the communication clean and professional.

```
EMAIL DISTRIBUTION:

PRIMARY RECIPIENT:
└── Your direct manager (always)

CC (OPTIONAL):
├── HR representative (if company policy requires)
├── Skip-level manager (for senior roles)
└── Admin/EA who handles logistics

DO NOT CC:
├── Your entire team
├── Other managers
├── External contacts
├── Anyone who shouldn't know before official announcement

TIMING:
Send immediately after your verbal conversation ends.
Don't wait days - it should be same-day documentation.
```

### Junior → FAANG: Resignation Letter as a Permanent Professional Record

Your resignation letter becomes part of your employment file and is referenced for future background checks and reference requests. Keep it professional, brief, and positive. It should contain: the date, a clear statement of your resignation and your last day, a sentence of genuine gratitude, and a brief offer to help with the transition. Nothing else. No grievances, no detailed explanation, no excessive apology.

The letter is a legal document as well as a professional one. It establishes the date of your resignation and your agreed last day, which matters for equity vesting, benefits continuation, and severance calculations if applicable. Keep a copy for your records.

### Senior @ FAANG: Resignation Letter Addendum and Transition Agreement at Senior Level

At senior level, the resignation letter may be supplemented by a separate transition agreement or notice period arrangement. If you are negotiating an extended notice period, confirming a specific transition plan, or agreeing to any post-employment consulting arrangement, get these agreements in writing signed by HR, not just acknowledged verbally by your manager.

The transition agreement is particularly important for senior engineers who may be asked to provide post-employment assistance. Understand what you are agreeing to, for how long, and whether you will be compensated. Informal agreements to "just email if you have questions" can become significant time obligations if not scoped explicitly.
