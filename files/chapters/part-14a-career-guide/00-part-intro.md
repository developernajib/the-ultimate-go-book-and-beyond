# Part XIV: Career Guide

*Your career is not a ladder, it is a jungle gym. The best moves are not always up.*

---

Technical skill alone does not get you hired at a FAANG company, and it certainly does not get you promoted once you are there. The engineers who land the best roles and advance the fastest are those who treat their career with the same rigor they apply to system design - they plan strategically, execute deliberately, and optimize continuously. They know how to build a compelling professional presence, navigate multi-round interview processes, negotiate offers worth hundreds of thousands of dollars, and position themselves for promotion within complex organizational structures.

This is the part of software engineering that nobody teaches in computer science programs or coding bootcamps. How do you get a referral at Google? What should your first 90 days at a new company look like? When is the right time to push for promotion versus switching companies for a level bump? How do you negotiate competing offers without burning bridges? These questions have concrete, actionable answers - and getting them wrong can cost you years of career progress and hundreds of thousands of dollars in lifetime earnings.

This part is your complete career playbook, covering everything from building your professional foundation to long-term career strategy. Whether you are preparing for your first FAANG application or planning your trajectory from senior engineer to engineering director, these chapters provide the frameworks, tactics, and insider knowledge that make the difference between a good career and an exceptional one.

**What this part covers:**
- Chapter 91: Building Your Foundation - crafting your CV, optimizing LinkedIn, building a GitHub portfolio, and establishing professional credibility
- Chapter 92: Job Search Strategy - using referrals, cold applications, recruiter outreach, and timing your search for maximum advantage
- Chapter 93: The Interview Process - phone screens, technical rounds, system design interviews, and behavioral evaluation from start to offer
- Chapter 94: Your First 90 Days - onboarding strategy, building relationships, shipping early wins, and establishing your reputation
- Chapter 95: Performance & Promotion - understanding promotion criteria, building a promotion packet, and managing your performance narrative
- Chapter 96: Long-Term Career Strategy - IC versus management track, specialization versus generalization, and decade-scale career planning
- Chapter 96B: Offer & Negotiation Mastery - understanding total compensation, negotiating competing offers, and maximizing your package
- Chapter 96C: Resignation & Transition - leaving gracefully, managing counteroffers, and maintaining professional relationships
- Chapter 96D: Relocation & Immigration - international opportunities, visa sponsorship, and navigating cross-border career moves

**How to use this part:**
If you are actively job searching, start with chapters 91 through 93 for immediate tactical value. If you are already employed and focused on growth, chapters 94 through 96 address onboarding, performance, and career planning. Chapters 96B through 96D cover transitions and can be read when relevant. The entire part is designed to be both a sequential guide and a reference you return to at each career inflection point.

**FAANG relevance:**
FAANG companies have highly structured interview processes and promotion systems. Understanding how these systems work - what interviewers are calibrated to look for, how promotion committees evaluate impact, and how compensation bands are structured - gives you a significant advantage over engineers who rely solely on technical ability. This part decodes those systems and gives you the playbook to navigate them successfully.
