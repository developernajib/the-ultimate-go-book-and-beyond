## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:
- Create a resume that passes both ATS and human review
- Optimize your LinkedIn profile for recruiters
- Build a GitHub profile that showcases your skills
- Develop a portfolio that demonstrates real impact
- Present a consistent professional brand across platforms

### Junior → FAANG: What These Objectives Mean for Your First Job Search

Each objective in this list represents a skill gap that costs junior candidates interviews. Resume writing is not intuitive for engineers because school projects are evaluated on correctness, but resumes are evaluated on communicated impact. The fastest way to close this gap is to write your first draft and then have a senior engineer read it without context and tell you what they infer about your level and impact. If their inference does not match reality, your materials are not doing their job.

LinkedIn optimization feels like a low-priority task but it is the primary channel through which FAANG recruiters make first contact. If your headline still reads "Software Engineer" with no specificity, you are invisible in every recruiter search that includes technology or domain terms. Change this before anything else.

### Senior @ FAANG: Reframing Objectives for Bar-Raiser Evaluation

These objectives apply differently at senior level. A staff engineer is not trying to "showcase skills" on GitHub. They are demonstrating technical leadership: the ability to set direction, make principled tradeoffs, and communicate the reasoning to a broad audience including non-engineers. The GitHub objective at senior level becomes "build public artifacts that show how you think about system design at scope," not "prove you can write code."

The "consistent professional brand" objective scales into platform strategy at L6 and above. Your brand is not just what you say about yourself. It is the observable record of where you have spoken, what you have written, which standards bodies or open-source projects you have influenced, and who in the industry references your work. Building this record intentionally over a two to three year period before a senior job search dramatically increases both inbound recruiter contact quality and the level at which you are assessed during the interview process.
