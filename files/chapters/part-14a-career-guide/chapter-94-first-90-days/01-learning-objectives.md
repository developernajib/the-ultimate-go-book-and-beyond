## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:
- Create a structured plan for your first 90 days
- Build relationships and understand the organization
- Deliver early wins while learning
- Avoid common new hire mistakes
- Set yourself up for long-term success

### Junior → FAANG: What "Long-Term Success" Actually Requires in Year One

The objectives in this list assume that long-term success is the natural result of avoiding mistakes and delivering early wins. At FAANG companies, long-term success in year one requires one additional explicit action: building visibility with your manager and skip-level manager about the quality and scope of your work.

Engineers who do good work that is invisible to leadership are consistently passed over for the most interesting projects and the fastest promotion timelines. Visibility is not self-promotion in the pejorative sense. It is the regular communication of impact: what you shipped, what got better, and what you are working on next. Establishing this communication pattern in the first 90 days sets up the feedback loops that drive growth in months four through twelve.

### Senior @ FAANG: Reframing Objectives for Staff-Level Entry

At senior level, the objective "create a structured plan for your first 90 days" is not a personal action plan. It is a stakeholder communication artifact. A staff engineer joining a new team should produce a written 30-60-90 day plan in the first week, share it with their manager and key stakeholders, and use it as a coordination mechanism. This signals operating maturity: you are treating your onboarding as a project to be managed, not a passive experience to be had.

The objective "build relationships and understand the organization" at senior level translates into explicit stakeholder mapping: who are the decision-makers in adjacent domains whose buy-in you will need for the technical changes you are likely to drive, and what is each person's current relationship with the team you are joining. This mapping should exist as a written artifact that you update over the first 60 days. It is the foundation of the influence network you will need to operate effectively at senior level in the organization.
