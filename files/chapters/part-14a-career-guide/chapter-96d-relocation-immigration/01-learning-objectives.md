## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:
- Negotiate full relocation packages
- Understand US visa options for tech workers
- Navigate the green card process
- Evaluate domestic US relocation decisions
- Consider international relocation opportunities
- Make informed decisions about remote work vs relocation

### Junior → FAANG: Priority Objectives for Early-Career Relocation

The highest-priority objective for early-career engineers is understanding US visa options and the green card process, because these timelines affect job search strategy and company selection in ways that are not visible until you are already in the middle of a process. Engineers who start the green card process early in their first FAANG role typically have more career flexibility five years in than engineers who wait until they feel settled.

The relocation package negotiation objective is immediately actionable: do not accept a first offer on relocation package any more than you would accept a first offer on base salary. Ask specifically for itemized reimbursement, temporary housing, and if applicable, a cost of living adjustment for the transition period.

### Senior @ FAANG: Advanced Objectives for Senior Location Strategy

At senior level, the objectives in this section expand to include international employment law, tax treaty implications for equity compensation across borders, and the organizational dynamics of senior remote work. Each of these dimensions requires specialist knowledge (employment attorney, international tax advisor, experienced recruiter in the target market) that goes beyond what any single book chapter can provide. Use this chapter as a framework for knowing what questions to ask those specialists, not as a substitute for their advice.

The green card process objective at senior level is specifically time-sensitive for engineers who are early in the priority date backlog. Understanding your current priority date position, the expected timeline based on country of birth, and the job change implications for pending I-140 petitions should be maintained as a continuous awareness practice, not addressed only at job change moments.
