## Influential Blogs and Authors

### Dave Cheney

**Blog**: https://dave.cheney.net
**Twitter**: @davecheney
**GitHub**: https://github.com/davecheney

**Why Follow**: One of the most respected voices in the Go community. Former Go contributor, excellent technical writer.

**Must-Read Articles**:
- "Don't just check errors, handle them gracefully"
- "Constant errors"
- "SOLID Go Design"
- "Practical Go: Real world advice for writing maintainable Go programs"
- "High Performance Go Workshop"
- "Let's talk about logging"
- "Ice cream makers and data races"

**Themes**: Error handling, performance, API design, concurrency, simplicity.

**Resources**:
- High Performance Go Workshop materials
- Conference talks on YouTube

### Russ Cox

**Blog**: https://research.swtch.com
**Role**: Go tech lead at Google
**Twitter**: @_rsc

**Why Follow**: Deep technical posts on Go internals, language design, and computer science.

**Must-Read Series**:
- "Go Data Structures" series
- "Go's Module System" series
- "Regular Expression Matching" series
- Posts on Go's history and design decisions

**Themes**: Language design, implementation details, formal verification, history.

**Note**: Posts are deeply technical and require careful reading.

- "Go Concurrency Patterns" (slides)
- "Advanced Go Concurrency Patterns" (slides)
- "Lexical Scanning in Go" (video and article)
- "Regular Expressions in Go" (talk)

**Themes**: Concurrency, language philosophy, software design, history.
