## Podcasts

### Go Time

**URL**: https://changelog.com/gotime
**Hosts**: Various rotating hosts from the Go community
**Format**: Weekly podcast, ~1 hour episodes
**Cost**: Free

**Why Listen**: The most popular Go podcast. Discussions with Go team members, library authors, and production users.

**Episode Types**:
- **Technical Deep Dives**: Detailed discussions on Go features
- **Project Showcases**: Interesting Go projects and their stories
- **Community Discussions**: Best practices, opinions, and debates
- **Ask Me Anything**: Q&A with Go experts
- **Unpopular Opinions**: Fun segment on controversial Go topics

**Notable Episodes**:
- "Concurrency patterns" discussions
- Interviews with Go team members (Russ Cox, Ian Lance Taylor)
- Production war stories from companies
- New feature announcements and discussions

**Listening Strategy**:
- Subscribe and listen weekly for current topics
- Browse archives for specific topics
- Listen during commutes or workouts

**Community**: Active listener community on Slack and Twitter.

### Cup o' Go

**URL**: https://cupogo.dev
**Hosts**: Jonathan Hall and Shay Nehmad
**Format**: Weekly news and discussions, ~30 minutes
**Cost**: Free

**Why Listen**: Weekly news roundup of Go ecosystem updates, new packages, and community happenings.

**Format**:
- Go news and announcements
- Package of the week
- Community projects
- Tips and tricks

**Best For**: Staying current with Go ecosystem without spending hours reading.

**Frequency**: Weekly, perfect for regular listening.
