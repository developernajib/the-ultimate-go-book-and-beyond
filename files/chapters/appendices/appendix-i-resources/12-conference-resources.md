## Conference Resources

### GopherCon (US)

**URL**: https://www.gophercon.com
**Location**: San Diego, CA (typically)
**Frequency**: Annual (usually November/December)
**Size**: 1,500+ attendees

**Why Attend**: The original and largest Go conference. Keynotes from Go team members, networking, training workshops.

**Tracks**:
- Main conference talks
- Lightning talks
- Training workshops
- Sponsor exhibitions
- Social events

**Video Archive**: https://www.youtube.com/@GopherConEurope

**Notable Past Speakers**: Rob Pike, Russ Cox, Brad Fitzpatrick, Kelsey Hightower, many others.

**Tips for Attendees**:
- Book early (sells out)
- Attend workshops for hands-on learning
- Network in hallway track
- Watch lightning talks for community projects

### GopherCon Europe

**URL**: https://gophercon.eu
**Location**: Berlin, Germany (typically)
**Frequency**: Annual (usually June/July)
**Size**: 600+ attendees

**Why Attend**: European Go conference with excellent speakers and diverse perspectives.

**Format**: Similar to US GopherCon with talks, workshops, and networking.

### Speaking at Conferences

Want to speak at Go conferences?

**Tips**:
1. **Start Local**: Practice at local meetups
2. **Submit Early**: Call for Proposals (CFP) usually 3-6 months before
3. **Unique Perspective**: Share real production experiences
4. **Practice**: Rehearse multiple times
5. **Share Code**: Make examples available on GitHub

**CFP Resources**:
- Conference websites list CFP deadlines
- Twitter #golang hashtag announces CFPs
- Go Time podcast mentions CFPs
