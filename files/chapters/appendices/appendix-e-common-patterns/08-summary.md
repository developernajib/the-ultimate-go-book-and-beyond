## Summary

These six patterns form the foundation of production Go code:

1. **Functional Options**: Clean, extensible configuration APIs
2. **Worker Pool / Fan-out/Fan-in**: Controlled concurrency and parallel processing
3. **Circuit Breaker / Rate Limiter**: Service resilience and resource protection
4. **Error Wrapping / Sentinel Errors**: Reliable error handling and propagation
5. **Graceful Shutdown**: Clean service termination without data loss
6. **Table-Driven Tests**: Maintainable, thorough test coverage

These patterns appear repeatedly in production Go codebases. Study the implementations, adapt them to your specific needs, and combine them as required.
