### Exercise 2: Practice Recording and Feedback

**Task**: Record yourself answering these three questions. Watch the recordings and grade yourself.

**Questions**:
1. "Tell me about yourself" (2 minutes max)
2. "Tell me about your biggest failure"
3. "Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager"

**Self-Evaluation Rubric**:

```markdown
# Self-Evaluation Rubric

## Question 1: "Tell me about yourself"

### Structure (0-5 points)
- [ ] Present-Past-Future structure (2 pts)
- [ ] Under 2 minutes (1 pt)
- [ ] Connected to why this company (2 pts)

### Content (0-5 points)
- [ ] Clear current role description (1 pt)
- [ ] Key achievements mentioned (2 pts)
- [ ] Relevant experience highlighted (2 pts)

### Delivery (0-5 points)
- [ ] Confident but not arrogant (2 pts)
- [ ] Natural pace, no filler words (2 pts)
- [ ] Eye contact / camera engagement (1 pt)

### Score: ___/15

---

## Question 2: "Tell me about your biggest failure"

### Structure (0-5 points)
- [ ] Clear STAR format (2 pts)
- [ ] Genuine failure, not humble-brag (2 pts)
- [ ] Learning explicitly stated (1 pt)

### Content (0-5 points)
- [ ] Owned the failure completely (2 pts)
- [ ] Specific actions (not vague) (2 pts)
- [ ] Applied learning to subsequent situation (1 pt)

### Delivery (0-5 points)
- [ ] Reflective tone, not defensive (2 pts)
- [ ] Appropriate length (2-3 min) (2 pts)
- [ ] Genuine self-awareness shown (1 pt)

### Score: ___/15

---

## Question 3: "Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager"

### Structure (0-5 points)
- [ ] Clear STAR format (2 pts)
- [ ] Professional disagreement (not complaint) (2 pts)
- [ ] Resolution included (1 pt)

### Content (0-5 points)
- [ ] Specific disagreement topic (1 pt)
- [ ] Your reasoning explained (2 pts)
- [ ] How you communicated disagreement (2 pts)

### Delivery (0-5 points)
- [ ] Professional tone about manager (2 pts)
- [ ] Showed respect while disagreeing (2 pts)
- [ ] Demonstrated maturity (1 pt)

### Score: ___/15

---

## Total Score: ___/45

### Interpretation:
- 40-45: Ready for interviews
- 30-39: Good foundation, refine weak areas
- 20-29: Need more practice, identify patterns
- Below 20: Start with written preparation first

### Common Issues to Watch For:
1. Using "we" instead of "I"
2. No specific numbers/metrics
3. Rambling past 3 minutes
4. Defensive tone about failures
5. Not connecting to company values
```

### Junior → FAANG: Behavioral Practice Exercises

The most effective behavioral exercises are recorded practice sessions reviewed for content and delivery. Record yourself answering 5 behavioral questions without notes, then review: did you have specific examples for each, did the results include metrics, did you spend more than 60% of the time on the Action section, and were you under 3 minutes for each answer. Many candidates are surprised to find they use filler phrases, rush through the result, or speak unclearly under simulated pressure.

### Senior @ FAANG: Exercises as a Calibration Tool for Level-Appropriate Scope

Senior exercises should include peer calibration: have a colleague who works at your target level or above listen to your answers and give feedback on scope. The question to answer: do your stories sound like they belong to an L6 engineer or an L4 engineer? The proxy signals are: how many people were affected, what was the business metric, did you make the decision or did you implement someone else's decision, and did your work change how other teams operated. If your peer calibration reveals scope misalignment, the exercise is to reframe existing stories from the organizational perspective: not 'I built X' but 'I drove the initiative to adopt X across the backend platform, which required aligning three team leads and delivering training to 20 engineers, and resulted in a 15% reduction in incident rate.'
