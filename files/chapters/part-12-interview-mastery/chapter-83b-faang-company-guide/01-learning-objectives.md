## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Describe the complete interview process, timeline, and evaluation criteria for each FAANG+ company
- Tailor your coding style, communication approach, and problem-solving strategy to each company's expectations
- Solve representative interview problems for each company using idiomatic Go
- Articulate how Go is used at each target company and answer Go-specific infrastructure questions
- Map your experience level to the correct role level at each company and calibrate your preparation accordingly
- Execute a structured 12-week preparation plan targeting one or more specific companies
- Apply interview day strategies that maximize your performance across different interview environments


### Junior → FAANG: Setting Goals for Company-Specific Preparation

Learning objectives for company-specific preparation should be concrete: 'I can name the three most differentiating aspects of Google's system design bar compared to Amazon's' and 'I have practiced two mock interviews using Meta-style questions'. Vague objectives like 'understand company culture' are not actionable and do not tell you when you are ready.

### Senior @ FAANG: Company-Specific Objectives as a Preparation Prioritization Tool

Senior candidates targeting multiple companies face a prioritization problem: comprehensive preparation for all FAANG companies simultaneously is not feasible. Use your objectives to prioritize by interview date, by fit with your current experience, and by your weakest dimensions per company. If your system design is strong but your behavioral stories are thin on Amazon-specific LP coverage, allocate preparation time accordingly. Track your objectives in a preparation log so you can assess progress objectively rather than through the self-assessment bias that tends toward overconfidence.
