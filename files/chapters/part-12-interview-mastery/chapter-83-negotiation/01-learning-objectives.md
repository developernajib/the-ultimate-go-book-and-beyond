## Learning Objectives

Compensation negotiation is a skill that can be learned and practiced, just like coding or system design. The objectives below outline the specific capabilities you will develop by working through this chapter. Each objective maps to a section that provides both the conceptual understanding and the practical scripts you need.

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:
- Understand total compensation components at tech companies
- Research and determine your market value
- Negotiate base salary, equity, and signing bonuses effectively
- Handle multiple offers and use them appropriately
- Navigate counteroffers from your current employer
- Make informed decisions beyond just compensation


### Junior → FAANG: Negotiation Learning Objectives

The learning objectives for negotiation are concrete: understand the components of FAANG compensation, know how to research your market value before negotiating, learn the specific scripts and approaches for negotiation conversations, and know how to handle competing offers and counteroffers. These objectives require practice, not just reading. Role-play the negotiation conversation before doing it with a real recruiter.

### Senior @ FAANG: Negotiation Objectives at the Senior Level: Strategy Beyond Scripts

Senior candidates should add strategic negotiation objectives to the list: understand which compensation components are most negotiable at your target level and company (equity is often more negotiable than base salary at FAANG, signing bonuses are often negotiable when equity is not), know when to negotiate via email versus phone (email gives you time to think, phone gives recruiters less time to plan a counter), and understand the recruiter's constraints and incentives (recruiters have compensation bands, and their job is easier when you accept quickly within the band). Understanding the system you are negotiating within gives you more effective leverage than the negotiation scripts alone.
