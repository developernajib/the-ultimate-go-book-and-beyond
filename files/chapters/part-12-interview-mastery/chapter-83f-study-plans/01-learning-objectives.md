## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

1. Assess your current interview readiness across coding, system design, behavioral, and Go proficiency dimensions using the diagnostic framework
2. Select and customize the appropriate study plan based on your timeline, current level, and target companies
3. Execute a structured, day-by-day preparation schedule with specific problem assignments and measurable weekly milestones
4. Allocate preparation time optimally across the four interview pillars based on your target company and level
5. Track your progress using quantitative metrics and adjust your plan when you fall behind or ahead of schedule
6. Prepare for company-specific interview formats, evaluation criteria, and cultural expectations
7. Maintain physical health, mental resilience, and motivation throughout an extended preparation period
8. Transition from interview preparation to on-the-job performance during your first 90 days at a new company


### Junior → FAANG: Study Plan Learning Objectives

The learning objectives for the study plans chapter are: understand how to self-assess your current preparation level, choose the right study plan for your timeline, adapt the plan to your specific weak areas, and measure your progress against concrete milestones. Generic study plans are starting points, not blueprints. The value is in the adaptation to your specific situation.

### Senior @ FAANG: Objectives as a Measurement Framework

Senior candidates should translate the study plan chapter objectives into personal measurable outcomes: not 'improve system design' but 'be able to complete a distributed system design for a social media timeline with full capacity estimation, trade-off analysis, and failure mode discussion in 45 minutes without prompting'. These specific, measurable outcomes provide a clear readiness signal when achieved. Track them in a preparation log that you review weekly to adjust the plan based on actual progress.
