## 83F.12 Resource List and Tool Recommendations

### Books

| Book | Focus Area | When to Read | Priority |
|------|-----------|-------------|----------|
| **Designing Data-Intensive Applications (DDIA)** by Martin Kleppmann | System design foundations | Month 1-2 of system design prep | Essential |
| **Cracking the Coding Interview** by Gayle Laakmann McDowell | Coding interview overview and problems | Before starting any plan (orientation) | Recommended |
| **System Design Interview Vol 1 & 2** by Alex Xu | System design case studies | During system design phase | Essential |
| **Elements of Programming Interviews** by Aziz, Lee, Prakash | Advanced algorithm practice | During Weeks 5-6 (advanced DSA) | Optional |
| **The Go Programming Language** by Donovan & Kernighan | Go mastery | Go refresher phase (Week 1-2 or Month 1) | Recommended |
| **Concurrency in Go** by Katherine Cox-Buday | Go concurrency depth | After Go basics, before system design | Recommended for L5+ |

### AI Tools for Practice

| Tool | Use Case | How to Use Effectively |
|------|---------|----------------------|
| **Claude** | Mock behavioral interviews, solution review, concept explanation | Ask Claude to act as an interviewer. Present your solution and ask for critique. |
| **ChatGPT** | Solution explanation, alternative approaches | Paste a problem and your solution, ask "What am I missing?" |
| **GitHub Copilot** | Disabled during practice, useful for production code | Turn OFF during interview prep to build unassisted coding muscle |

**Warning:** AI tools are supplements, not replacements. Solving a problem "with AI help" does not count as solving it. Use AI for review, explanation, and mock interviews - not for generating solutions you then memorize.

### Junior → FAANG: Interview Preparation Resource Recommendations

The most commonly recommended resources for FAANG preparation: LeetCode (coding practice), the System Design Primer on GitHub (system design fundamentals), Designing Data-Intensive Applications by Martin Kleppmann (distributed systems depth), and Interviewing.io or Pramp (mock interviews with real engineers). For behavioral preparation, Amazon's 16 Leadership Principles documentation and company engineering blog posts are the most relevant. Avoid over-investing in video courses for topics you can learn through active practice. Passive watching is low-efficiency preparation.

### Senior @ FAANG: Resource Selection as a Senior-Level Curation Challenge

Senior candidates face a curation challenge: there is more preparation material available than time allows, and not all material is calibrated for the L6 bar. The selection criteria for senior-level resources: Is this calibrated for L6 or above? Does it include trade-off analysis and failure mode discussion, not just high-level diagrams? Does the author have genuine FAANG interview experience as an interviewer (not just as a candidate)? Resources that meet these criteria are rare and worth prioritizing. Academic papers from Google (Bigtable, Spanner, Borg, Chubby), Meta (Cassandra, Scribe), and Amazon (Dynamo, Chord) provide depth on the distributed systems problems that FAANG companies actually solved, which is the level of specificity that L6 system design bar-raisers respect.
