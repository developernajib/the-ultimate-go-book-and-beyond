### Junior → FAANG: What Comes After Interview Day Mastery

After completing this chapter, you have the tools to prepare for and execute FAANG interviews at any level. The next steps depend on where you are in the process: if you have not started preparation yet, build your study plan and begin. If you are mid-preparation, assess your progress against the study plan and adjust. If you are post-interview, run the post-interview debrief protocol and decide on next steps. The work does not end with an offer. Negotiation and first-90-days planning are the final phases of the process.

### Senior @ FAANG: What Comes Next at the Senior Level: From Candidate to Hiring Manager

Senior engineers who have completed a successful FAANG interview loop are positioned to pay it forward: as a bar-raiser interviewer, a mock interview partner, or a mentor for junior engineers preparing for their first FAANG attempt. The knowledge of the interview system from the candidate's perspective makes you a more calibrated interviewer. The governance concern at the staff level is maintaining interview quality over time: calibration sessions with your interviewer peers, reviewing how your ratings compare to the final hiring decisions, and proactively improving the interview questions and rubrics your team uses to ensure they are assessing the right signals at the right level.
